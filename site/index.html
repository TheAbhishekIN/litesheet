The spreadsheet that's
fast, light and private.

Features

Everything you reach for every day

The spreadsheet essentials, done well, in a small native app that opens instantly.

Your files, as they are

Open .xlsx, .xls, .ods, .csv and .tsv. Save back to .xlsx with formulas, dates and column widths kept.

400+ Excel functions

SUMIFS, VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, TEXT and many more, with live recalculation and references across sheets.

Built for big files

A virtualized grid draws only what's on screen, so 200,000 rows scroll as smoothly as 20.

Filter, sort, find

Column filters with conditions and value checklists, one-click sorting, and search that highlights every match.

Looks the part

Bold, colours, borders, merged cells, frozen headers and currency or date formats, all kept when you open and save Excel files.

Private by design

No account, telemetry or cloud. LiteSheet only goes online when you ask the AI something.

Formulas

Build formulas by pointing

Type =, then click or drag over cells to insert references, just like in Excel. The range you're pointing at is highlighted, and missing brackets are closed for you.

  • Relative references adjust when you copy, fill or sort
  • $A$1-style anchors stay put
  • Familiar errors: #DIV/0!, #N/A, #REF!
Data

Filters that make sense

Combine conditions like "greater than 200" with a checklist of values. Filtered rows are only hidden, never deleted, and the status bar shows how many rows you're looking at.

  • Equals, contains, starts with, >, ≤, is empty, and more
  • Right-click any cell to filter by its value
  • Arrow keys skip hidden rows
Charts

Charts that keep up

Turn a selection into a column, bar, line, area, pie or scatter chart with a live preview. Charts update as your numbers change, can be moved and resized on the sheet, and are saved as real Excel charts.

  • Series names and categories are detected for you
  • Link sheets with formulas like =SUMIF(Orders!C2:C49, A2, Orders!H2:H49)
AI, your way

Ask in plain English. Bring your own key.

Connect any OpenAI-compatible provider (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq) or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio. Your key stays in the macOS Keychain, and the panel shows exactly how much data each request sent.

Write formulas for you

Describe what you want, like "total revenue from shipped West region orders", and get a formula written for your columns, ready to insert.

Filter, explain, summarize

  • Filter: "West region orders over $300" becomes regular column filters you can inspect and edit
  • Explain: select a range and ask what it shows
  • Summarize: get the key figures of a whole sheet

Get started

Install in a minute

One download for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 11 or later.

Download

LiteSheet-macos.zip from the latest release, and double-click it to unzip.

Move to Applications

Drag LiteSheet.app into your Applications folder.

Allow it once

Open LiteSheet. When macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Why the extra step? LiteSheet is free and not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to approve it the first time. Instead of using System Settings, you can also run this once in Terminal: 
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LiteSheet.app
On macOS 14 and earlier, right-clicking the app and choosing Open also works. Prefer to build it yourself? Clone the repo and run cargo run --release. See the README.
