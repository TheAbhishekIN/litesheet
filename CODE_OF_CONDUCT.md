# Code of Conduct

LiteSheet adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The summary below doesn't replace it.

## Our pledge

We want LiteSheet to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory or discriminatory comments
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal and political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact a project maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue concerned. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
